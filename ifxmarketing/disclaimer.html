Disclaimer

What our marketing services do and do not cover, and where responsibility sits.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

1. Marketing services only

IFX Marketing provides advertising and marketing services. We are not a broker, broker-dealer, investment adviser, portfolio manager, research analyst or trading educator. We do not place trades, manage funds, or provide signals.

Nothing on this website, in a campaign we build, or in correspondence with us constitutes financial, investment, trading, tax, legal or regulatory advice. Decisions about trading, investment products and business structure are yours.

2. No guarantee of campaign outcomes

We do not guarantee any specific marketing result, including:

  • the number of impressions, clicks, leads or enquiries a campaign will produce;
  • the cost per lead, cost per acquisition or return on advertising spend;
  • the conversion of enquiries into clients, accounts, deposits or revenue;
  • approval, continued delivery or reinstatement of any advertisement, ad account or landing page by Meta, Google or any other platform;
  • any position, ranking or visibility in search engine results.

Advertising performance depends on factors outside our control, including your offer, pricing, market conditions, competitor activity, platform auction dynamics, landing page experience, sales follow-up and the quality of the product itself.

3. Illustrations are not client results

Any chart, dashboard graphic or metric shown on this website is illustrative of the kind of reporting and planning we do. Unless a page explicitly labels something as a verified result, nothing on this site should be read as a reported outcome for a client. No formal case studies are currently published because none are available for publication.

4. Client responsibility for compliance

Clients are responsible for ensuring that their own products, financial promotions, licences, registrations, disclosures and operations meet the requirements that apply to them — in the markets they serve and on the platforms they advertise on.

Where advertising rules require authorisation or verification, obtaining and maintaining it is the client's responsibility. We can plan campaigns with policy constraints in mind, but we cannot approve ads on a platform's behalf or certify regulatory compliance.

5. Third-party platforms

Meta, Google, WhatsApp and other platforms operate independently. Their policies, targeting options, pricing and approval processes can change without notice, and their decisions are final within their own systems. We are not responsible for those decisions.

6. Information accuracy

We aim for accuracy in what we publish. Where a figure requires verification, we either verify it or describe the business in neutral terms instead. Business details such as team size and founding year reflect information supplied by the business.

If you spot something inaccurate on this site, please tell us at connect.ifx@gmail.com and we will correct it.

7. Trading risk

Trading forex, CFDs, derivatives and other leveraged instruments carries a high level of risk and can result in the loss of all invested capital. Nothing in our marketing services or on this website should be interpreted as an invitation to trade or as an assessment of suitability for any person.

8. External links

Links to external websites, platforms or profiles are provided for convenience. We do not control their content and are not responsible for it.
