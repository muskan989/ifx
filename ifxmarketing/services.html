Pricing

How we quote

Two models, chosen to suit the shape of the account:

  • Fixed monthly service fee. A predictable retainer for an agreed scope of work, independent of how spend scales within that scope.
  • Percentage of ad spend. The fee moves with media investment, which suits accounts planning to scale aggressively.

Media budget is always paid by you directly to Meta and Google. Landing page development and CRM setup are quoted as separate project deliverables where they are needed.

Before you enquire

  • Minimum monthly advertising budget of ₹1 lakh
  • A product you are licensed and permitted to promote
  • Someone available to follow up on enquiries
  • A clear idea of which market you want to target
