About IFX Marketing

A performance marketing agency built around trading and fintech

Established in 2017. A team of 10+ specialists running Meta Ads and Google Ads campaigns for forex brokers, algo trading businesses, advisory desks, introducing brokers, prop firms and fintech companies.

Company overview

Why the agency exists

Financial services advertising is not the same as advertising a consumer product. The audience is narrow, the language is technical, the platforms apply extra scrutiny to financial promotions, and a single poorly qualified enquiry can cost a sales desk more than a month of media spend.

IFX Marketing was set up in 2017 to work specifically inside that environment. Instead of learning a client's product from scratch each time, the team already knows how a broker's onboarding works, what an evaluation programme means for a prop firm, why an IB needs a different conversation from a retail trader, and which phrases in an ad will get an account restricted.

We work on paid acquisition: audience planning, campaign structure, creative, landing pages and the lead workflow that sits behind them. Everything is measured, and everything is discussed with the client in plain terms.

Our approach

How we think about fintech performance marketing

Five principles that shape how every campaign is planned and reviewed.

Clarity before spend

Before a campaign starts we agree what a good enquiry looks like. That definition drives the audience, the creative and the form fields — not the other way around.

Segmentation over scale

Trading audiences behave very differently by experience level and market. Separating them early makes performance readable and budget decisions defensible.

Conversion is a design decision

Form length, offer clarity, proof and page speed all affect enquiry quality. We treat landing pages as part of the campaign, not as an afterthought.

Policy-aware creative

Financial promotions attract extra review. Ad copy is written with platform policy and local advertising requirements in mind from the first draft.

Honest measurement

Platform metrics and business outcomes are reported separately. If a campaign is not performing, that is stated before it becomes a bigger problem.

No overpromising

We do not promise lead volumes, conversion rates, trading outcomes or rankings. We describe what we will test, how we will measure it and what we can control.

Platform experience

Meta Ads and Google Ads, managed in-house

Meta Ads is usually the primary channel for trading audiences in India: it reaches broad, interest-driven audiences at a comparatively low cost per impression, and gives creative room to explain a product. Google Ads works differently — it captures people already searching for a broker, a strategy or an evaluation programme, which typically means lower volume and higher intent.

Most campaigns we run combine both, with the split decided by the offer and the market rather than by habit. Because both platforms apply financial services advertising rules, account structure, copy and landing page content are planned together.

Advertising spend figures are only published once the business has verified them. This website does not currently publish a campaign-spend total.
