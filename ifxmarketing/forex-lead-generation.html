Broker marketing requirements

What a broker campaign has to get right

Forex marketing is constrained in ways most financial campaigns are not. The product is regulated differently in every market, the major ad platforms apply additional review to financial promotions, and the audience is small enough that careless targeting burns budget quickly.

A broker campaign therefore has to answer four questions before spend starts: which market you are licensed to serve, which trader profile you want, what the first conversion actually is, and who responds to that enquiry. Get those wrong and the campaign produces volume without value.

We plan broker acquisition around those four questions, then build the ad account and the landing page to match.

What we establish first

  • Licensed markets — where the broker can legally accept clients
  • Trader profile — new to trading, experienced, or high-deposit
  • Primary conversion — demo, live account, or a call with the desk
  • Follow-up capacity — how quickly enquiries are contacted

Audience targeting

Segmenting a narrow audience properly

India-focused acquisition

Broad reach on Meta combined with language and region splits, creative that reflects local trading habits, and Google search campaigns for broker-comparison and platform queries. Messaging avoids return claims and is written to survive compliance review.

International acquisition

Market-by-market campaigns where the language, currency, regulator and platform rules change. We do not duplicate one campaign across countries and hope the targeting holds.

Segmentation by experience

Someone learning what leverage means needs a different message from a trader comparing spreads across three brokers. Separate creative and landing pages make both perform better and make enquiry quality readable.

Retargeting audiences

Website visitors, video viewers and partial form completions are re-engaged with a different message rather than the same ad served again.

Meta Ads and Google Ads

Two platforms, two different jobs

Meta Ads for brokers

  • Creative-led campaigns across Facebook and Instagram
  • Interest, behaviour and lookalike audience layers
  • Video and static creative testing in structured cycles
  • Lead form or landing page routes, depending on follow-up speed
  • Copy reviewed against financial services advertising rules

Google Ads for brokers

  • Search campaigns on commercial-intent broker queries
  • Brand protection and competitor-term review
  • Negative keyword hygiene to cut wasted clicks
  • Display and YouTube for reach where the offer suits it
  • Conversion tracking on the action that matters to your desk

Platform approval is decided by Meta and Google, not by the agency. We cannot guarantee that any particular ad, account or landing page will be approved, and requirements can change without notice.

Lead forms and landing pages

Where the click becomes an enquiry

Single-purpose pages

One offer, one action. The page explains what the broker is, which markets it serves and what happens after the enquiry, without a navigation menu competing for attention.

Form design

Field count is a deliberate trade-off: enough to qualify, few enough to keep completion practical on a phone. Multi-step forms are used where qualification needs more detail.

Mobile performance

Most paid traffic arrives on a phone. Pages are built mobile-first, kept light, and checked at real screen widths before launch.

Landing page development is a separate deliverable from ad management. Existing pages can be used where they are already fast, clear and tracked correctly.

Lead qualification

Volume is not the objective

Qualification before the click

  • Audience definitions tied to trading behaviour
  • Creative that states the product plainly, including risk language
  • Separate paths for demo and live-account intent
  • Market-specific messaging rather than one global ad

Qualification after the click

  • Form questions on experience or intended deposit, where appropriate
  • Response workflow agreed with your desk before launch
  • Lead routing so enquiries reach the right person
  • Regular review of enquiry quality, not just enquiry count

A cheap enquiry that never answers the phone costs more than an expensive one that opens an account. We review both sides of that equation with you.

Tracking and optimisation

How performance gets measured

Tracking setup

Pixel and tag installation, conversion events for the actions that matter, and form-submission tracking so platform numbers can be checked against your own records. Events are tested before launch.

Optimisation cycle

Structured testing of audiences and creative, budget reallocation based on observed cost per qualified enquiry, and documented changes. Optimisation is incremental work, not a single setting.

Reporting

Spend, reach, click and conversion metrics reported alongside enquiry quality feedback from your team. Where a campaign underperforms, that is stated rather than reframed.

What we do not promise

Specific lead volumes, cost per lead, conversion rates, deposit values or trading outcomes. Those depend on factors outside any agency's control, including your offer, pricing and sales follow-up.

FAQs

Forex Lead Generation questions

Which markets do you run forex campaigns in?

India is our primary market, and we also run campaigns for international markets. Targeting, creative and platform policy requirements differ by country, so each market is planned separately rather than scaled from a single campaign.

Does Meta allow forex broker advertising?

Meta restricts certain financial products and requires advertisers to be authorised where applicable. Approval depends on the product, the targeting country, the landing page content and the account history, and it can change at any time. We plan campaigns with those constraints in mind but cannot guarantee that a specific ad will be approved.

How do you reduce low-quality enquiries?

By qualifying earlier: tighter audience definitions, clearer creative about what the product is and is not, and enquiry forms that ask about market experience or intended deposit where appropriate. We then review enquiry quality with your team and adjust.

Do you build the landing page as well?

Yes, where it is needed. Landing page development is a separate deliverable from ad management and is quoted as a project. Existing pages can be used if they are fast, clear and tracked correctly.

Can you guarantee a number of leads per month?

No. Lead volume depends on budget, offer, market conditions and platform approval. We can describe the testing plan and the metrics we will report, but we do not guarantee lead volumes or cost per lead.

Do you handle tracking and conversion setup?

Yes — pixel or tag installation, conversion events and form-submission tracking so campaign performance can be judged against your own records. Account and website access is arranged before the campaign build.
