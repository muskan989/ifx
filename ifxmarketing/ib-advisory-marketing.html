Introducing broker marketing

Two audiences, two separate campaigns

Introducing broker businesses sit between a broker and a trader, which means marketing has to work at two levels at once. On one side there is partner recruitment: convincing people who already have a trading audience that your commission structure, support and platform are worth promoting. On the other there is trader acquisition: reaching people who will trade through your link or your desk.

These are not the same conversation. Partner recruitment is a business proposition judged on payout terms, reporting and support. Trader acquisition is judged on the trading experience, the platform and the credibility of the brand behind it.

We build separate campaigns, separate landing pages and separate qualification paths for each, so reporting can show which side is producing what.

What we establish first

  • Which side needs growth — partners, traders, or both
  • Partner proposition — commission structure and support
  • Trader proposition — platform, spreads, service model
  • Routing — who responds to each type of enquiry

Trading advisory marketing

Selling a service without promising outcomes

Method over claims

Advisory and research services are marketed on process, coverage and transparency — what is analysed, how often, and who it suits. Return claims are avoided.

Credibility signals

Track record disclosure done properly, team experience described factually, and clear explanation of what the service does not do.

Audience fit

Campaigns target traders whose experience and capital level match the service, which improves both enquiry quality and retention.

IFX Marketing does not provide financial, investment, legal or regulatory advice. Clients are responsible for the compliance of their own promotions and disclosures.

Audience segmentation

Who each campaign is actually for

Aspiring partners

People with an existing trading audience — community admins, educators, signal providers — evaluating partnership programmes.

Experienced traders

Traders with capital who want a relationship rather than a platform, and who judge an advisory service on process and communication.

Newer traders

People early in their trading journey, best served by education-led messaging that is honest about the learning curve.

Regional partner networks

Localised partner recruitment where language, payment methods and support expectations shift by market.

Lead capture and qualification

Forms that separate partners from traders

Capture structure

  • Separate landing pages for partner and trader journeys
  • Routing field or separate forms so enquiries reach the right desk
  • Qualification questions on audience size for partner enquiries
  • Experience and capital indicators for trader enquiries
  • WhatsApp or CRM handoff so response is immediate

Qualification criteria

  • Partner: existing audience, markets covered, promotion method
  • Trader: experience level, account size range, service expectation
  • Advisory: preferred communication and time zone
  • Both: clarity on what the enquiry is asking for

Routing and workflow support is limited to systems you already operate. Where no CRM exists, we can recommend a structure but do not supply software licences.

Campaign measurement

What gets reviewed each month

Enquiry split by type

Partner and trader enquiries tracked separately, so budget can move toward whichever side the business needs.

Cost per qualified enquiry

Cost measured against enquiries that met the agreed criteria, rather than against total form fills.

Lead progression

Whether enquiries moved forward — partner conversations started, trader accounts opened — reported alongside platform metrics.

Creative performance

Which messages and formats produced quality rather than volume, reviewed before scaling any ad set.

Platform policy considerations

Financial promotions get extra scrutiny

Typical constraints

  • Financial services authorisation checks on ad accounts
  • Restrictions on return, profit and accuracy claims
  • Limited targeting options for financial products in some countries
  • Landing page review, including disclosure requirements
  • Referral and partnership claims that need to be substantiated

How we plan around them

  • Copy reviewed before build, not after rejection
  • Disclosure language placed on the landing page as well as the ad
  • Account structure that isolates higher-risk creative
  • Documented claims so they can be evidenced if challenged
  • No guarantee of approval — final decisions rest with the platforms

Regulatory requirements for IB and advisory businesses vary by jurisdiction. Clients are responsible for confirming what applies to them.

FAQs

IB & Advisory Marketing questions

What is the difference between IB marketing and broker marketing?

Broker marketing is aimed at traders opening an account directly. IB marketing has two audiences: partners who will introduce clients, and traders acquired through an IB network. The messages, landing pages and qualification criteria for those two groups are different.

Do you run partner recruitment campaigns?

Yes. Partner recruitment campaigns target people who already promote or advise traders, and use landing pages that explain commission structure, support and onboarding rather than trading features.

How do you market a trading advisory service without making performance claims?

By focusing on process, methodology, transparency and the type of trader the service suits. Claims about returns, accuracy or profit are avoided entirely, which is both a policy requirement and a credibility advantage.

Which platforms work for IB and advisory campaigns?

Meta Ads is usually the primary channel for reach and creative explanation, with Google Ads capturing people already searching for advisory services or partnership programmes. The split is decided per market.

Can enquiries be split by type automatically?

Yes, where a CRM or form workflow supports it. Partner enquiries and trader enquiries can be routed differently, using separate forms or a single form with a routing field.

Do you provide regulatory advice?

No. IFX Marketing provides marketing services only. Clients remain responsible for their licences, disclosures and the compliance of their own promotions.
