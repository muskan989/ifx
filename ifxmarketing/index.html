Trust and experience

A specialist agency, not a generalist media buyer

IFX Marketing was established in 2017 and has spent those years working on paid acquisition for trading-related and financial services businesses. That means the team already understands the vocabulary of the sector — spreads, lots, evaluations, IB structures, advisory mandates — and how those products have to be described within advertising platform rules.

The team of 10+ specialists covers campaign strategy, media buying, ad creative, landing page development and lead handling. Campaigns run on Meta Ads and Google Ads, with landing pages and enquiry flows built around the specific offer rather than a generic template.

Hands-on experience planning, buying and optimising Meta and Google Ads campaigns for trading and fintech businesses since 2017.

What that experience means in practice

  • Sector-aware creative. Ad copy written for trading and fintech audiences, with platform policy constraints factored in from the first draft.
  • Realistic audience models. Targeting built around trading behaviour and intent signals rather than broad finance interests.
  • Enquiry quality over volume. Form fields and qualification steps chosen to reduce time-wasting enquiries.
  • Measurement you can review. Campaign reporting that separates platform metrics from actual enquiry outcomes.

FAQs

Questions businesses ask before starting

Which industries does IFX Marketing serve?

We work with forex brokers, algorithmic trading businesses, trading advisory and research companies, introducing brokers, prop firms and broader fintech startups. Our paid advertising work is concentrated on trading-related and financial services businesses, where audience targeting and advertising policy requirements are specific.

Which advertising platforms do you manage?

Primarily Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) and Google Ads, including search, display and YouTube where relevant. Depending on the campaign we may also recommend platform-appropriate landing pages and lead-handling workflows rather than adding more channels without a clear purpose.

What is the recommended minimum advertising budget?

We generally work with businesses that can commit ₹1 lakh or more per month in advertising spend. Below that level there is often not enough data to test audiences and creatives properly, which makes optimisation slow and results harder to read.

How does your pricing work?

We quote either a fixed monthly service fee or a percentage of advertising spend, depending on scope and how the account will be structured. Both models are discussed transparently before work begins, and the fee is always separate from the media budget paid to Meta or Google.

Do you create landing pages?

Yes. Campaign-specific landing pages and multi-step enquiry flows are part of the scope when they are needed. This is a separate deliverable from ad management, and the scope, timeline and cost are agreed before the campaign starts.

How do you approach lead quality?

Lead quality is addressed at three levels: audience and offer definition, the fields and steps used in the enquiry form, and what happens after a lead arrives. We review enquiry quality with you during the engagement and adjust targeting, creative and form requirements accordingly. We do not guarantee a specific lead-to-client conversion rate.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. Our primary market is India, and we also work with international clients. Targeting, ad copy and platform policy requirements vary by country, so campaigns are planned per market rather than reused across geographies.

How can a business request a consultation?

Use the enquiry form on this page or the contact page, email connect.ifx@gmail.com, or message us on WhatsApp at +44 7446 345817. Share your business type and approximate monthly advertising budget so the first conversation can focus on campaign fit.

Are advertising results guaranteed?

No. Paid advertising outcomes depend on factors outside any agency's control, including the offer, pricing, market conditions, landing page experience, sales follow-up and platform auction dynamics. We do not guarantee leads, sales, profits or advertising approval, and we do not promise specific search rankings.
