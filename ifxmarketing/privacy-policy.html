Privacy Policy

How IFX Marketing handles the limited information this website collects, and what happens when you contact us.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Draft document — legal review required. This policy describes how the website actually works, but it has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Matters marked [TO CONFIRM] need business-specific input before publication. Do not assume this document creates compliance with GDPR, Indian law, UAE law or any other regime until a qualified adviser has reviewed it.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by IFX Marketing (“we”, “us”), a fintech performance marketing agency established in 2017. Our contact address for privacy matters is connect.ifx@gmail.com.

[TO CONFIRM] Registered legal entity name, registration number, registered office address and any data-protection officer or representative appointment. These details were not supplied and are deliberately not invented here.

2. What this website collects

The short answer is: very little, and none of it automatically except standard server logs.

  • Enquiry form data. If you complete the enquiry form, you enter your name, phone number, business type and monthly advertising budget. This information is processed in your browser only. It is not transmitted to, or stored on, a server belonging to this website.
  • WhatsApp handoff. When you submit the form, your browser opens WhatsApp with a pre-written message containing those details. If you press Send in WhatsApp, the message is delivered through WhatsApp, which is operated by Meta. At that point the information is handled under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy, not ours.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records standard technical information when a page is requested, such as IP address, browser type, requested page and timestamp. This is normal for any website and is used for security and reliability.
  • Direct contact. If you email us or message us on WhatsApp or Instagram, we receive whatever you choose to send, together with the contact details of the account you used.

3. What this website does not do

  • No analytics or advertising trackers are loaded by default. There is no Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or equivalent installed in this build.
  • No cookies are set by this website itself.
  • No account creation, login, payment processing or file upload exists on this website.
  • Form submissions are not stored in a database and are not emailed to us automatically.

If analytics or advertising tracking is added later, this policy must be updated and, where required, consent obtained before any non-essential script runs. The configuration file contains an analytics.enabled flag that is currently false.

4. Why we process your information

When you contact us, we use the details you provide for one purpose: to respond to your enquiry and discuss whether our services are a fit for your business. We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to a marketing list as a result of an enquiry.

[TO CONFIRM] The legal basis relied on for processing (for example consent, legitimate interests, or contract negotiation) depends on the jurisdictions you operate in and requires legal confirmation.

5. Sharing

We do not share enquiry information with third parties for their own marketing. Information is handled by the service providers involved in the channels you choose to use:

  • WhatsApp / Meta — when you send a message through WhatsApp.
  • Email provider — the provider hosting the mailbox at connect.ifx@gmail.com.
  • Instagram — if you contact us there.
  • Hosting provider — for server logs and delivery of the site.

[TO CONFIRM] Names and locations of hosting and email providers, and whether any data transfer crosses borders in a way that requires additional safeguards.

6. Retention

Enquiry conversations are kept only as long as needed to respond and, if we begin working together, for the duration of the business relationship and any period required by applicable record-keeping rules.

[TO CONFIRM] A specific retention period should be set in writing — for example, enquiries that do not progress being deleted after a defined number of months.

7. Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it, by writing to connect.ifx@gmail.com. Because the website form does not store data, we will only hold information you have sent us directly through email, WhatsApp or Instagram.

You can also simply choose not to press Send in WhatsApp — nothing reaches us until you do.

8. Children

This website is intended for business audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Security

The website is served over HTTPS. Because the enquiry form does not transmit data to a server, there is no stored form data to protect. Messages sent through WhatsApp or email are protected by those providers' security arrangements, which we do not control.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. Material changes affecting how enquiries are handled will be reflected in the form's notice text.
